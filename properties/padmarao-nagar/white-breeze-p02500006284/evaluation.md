## What would change the decision

1. Obtain the current **GHMC occupancy certificate**, any RERA extension/amendment, and a dated construction/occupancy update. Inspect the built property against the sanctioned plan before accepting a ready-to-move claim.
2. Obtain the selected flat's **RERA carpet area**, builder brochure/floor plan, written all-in quote, proposed sale-deed area, and exact parking allotment. Recalculate all-in ₹/ft² of carpet and carpet-to-marketed efficiency then.
3. Have a qualified local property lawyer reconcile the **title report's land area and property-number differences** with the deeds, EC, GHMC records, mortgage release, and seller's right to convey.
4. At a visit, test the **single lift and stair fallback**, lift backup power, water source and monthly charges, bathroom clearances, ambulance access to the entrance, actual occupancy, and maintenance collection. For an elderly household, these operational checks are decisive.

**Completion boundary:** Every framework criterion has been addressed above, but the evaluation is not a verified purchase clearance. Current occupancy/legal status, selected-unit carpet and all-in price, actual building systems, walking and driving times, project-specific reviews, registered comparables, and resident operations cannot be closed from the available sources. Those gaps are explicit reasons for the hold decision, rather than silently assumed favorable findings.
