# Evaluation — GAMUT s VASAVI MEHER NIWAS

**Decision: hold pending evidence** · **24 September 2026** · Padmarao Nagar / Walker Town · Telangana RERA **P02500002671**. This is a desk assessment for a household including someone in their seventies, not legal or engineering clearance. [Official certificate](sources/tgrera-registration-certificate-2021-03-06.pdf) identifies `GAMUT s VASAVI MEHER NIWAS`, promoter **GAMUT INDIA PROJECTS**, Hyderabad 500025; the project has no separately verified phase label. Its signed registration runs to **11 Dec 2026**. The latest retrieved [detailed filing](sources/tgrera-detailed-filing-2023-06-15.html) was last modified **15 Jun 2023**, so its progress and booking fields are historical. The exact identity and source links are in [sources.md](sources.md); calculations and conflicts are in [rera-findings.md](rera-findings.md).

**Why hold:** The dated filing cannot establish present completion or occupancy. It disagrees with the signed certificate about proposed completion (**Apr 2027 vs Dec 2026**) and the sanctioned plan about registered flat count (**35 vs 36**). The brochure provides two lifts and backup as claims, but the 2023 filing still recorded lift work at 0%. The specific flat's carpet area, all-in quote, title/share allocation and parking entitlement are absent. A site visit is worthwhile **after** the builder supplies a current filing/OC status and identifies an offered flat.

Status terms: **verified** = present in the linked original or official operator record (often a dated historical fact); **claimed** = brochure/listing; **conflicting** = sources differ; **unavailable** = required evidence not obtained; **needs visit** = physical or operational observation. Each row names the evidence and date or exact follow-up.

## Evidence that would change the decision

1. **Current RERA QPR and GHMC OC/status**, plus the formal explanation for **Dec 2026 vs Apr 2027** and **35 vs 36** flats.
2. A **flat-specific pack**: RERA carpet and approved area schedule, written all-in cost sheet, draft deed, landowner share/allocation deed, updated EC, title opinion, mortgage release and assigned parking bay.
3. A **weekday site check** of both lifts, 8:30am waits, power-failure coverage, water bills, 6pm ambulance route, accessibility of the actual bathroom, occupied-flat count and 6:30pm community.
4. **3am/5pm airport routes** and walks to the chosen metro, grocery, chemist and temple, recorded from the exact gate.

If the current documents show an OC and an offered flat with a clear title/area/parking chain, move to **visit**. If the promoter cannot reconcile the approval and area differences or the operational checks fail for an elderly resident, **deprioritize**.
