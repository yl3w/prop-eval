## Site capacity and parking

185 filing flats / 3.285 net acres = **56.3 flats/net acre** (or 180 brochure flats / 3.285 = **54.8**). These figures assume this RERA parcel exclusively serves the five blocks; confirm sanctioned site boundaries and any shared facilities. The filing lists **5,437 m² open parking** and **zero covered parking bays**; its per-block “total parking area” fields are in m², not bay counts. Therefore parking entitlement, marked spaces and visitor/clubhouse sharing cannot be calculated. No commercial component is shown in the supplied project description/plan, but sanctioned use and physical sharing need confirmation.
