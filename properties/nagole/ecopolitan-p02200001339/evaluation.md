# Ecopolitan, Nagole — evaluation

**Decision: hold pending evidence (2026-09-24).** The user-supplied RERA printout and brochure match on name, promoter, Nagole/Bandlaguda location and (via the brochure) P02200001339. The location has an operating metro terminus and a plausible emergency hospital, and the low rise form is worth a visit. But the five-flat difference in Block 1, elapsed completion dates, unseen OC/extension documents, and lack of a selected flat's registered carpet area and all-in price prevent a purchase judgment. This is a research screen, not a legal or engineering clearance. The user asked to skip looking up another RERA filing or brochure and to skip Google reviews.

### What would change the decision

1. Obtain current **RERA registration/extension certificate**, actual **GHMC OC** and sanctioned as-built plan; reconcile the **185 versus 180** flat discrepancy, especially Eco I.
2. Choose a flat and obtain its **registered carpet area**, approved plan, owner/allocation and mortgage release, written **all-in quote**, sale-deed area basis and parking allotment. Then calculate all-in ₹/ft² of registered carpet and carpet/marketed efficiency.
3. Visit with the intended residents: inspect lift backup and 8:30 a.m. waits, water and DG bills, bathroom/ambulance access, 6:30 p.m. occupancy, rain-affected approach, and timed metro/errand/hospital routes. Ask the association for maintenance accounts and occupied-flat count.
