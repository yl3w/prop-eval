# ARIA, Nagole — property evaluation

**Decision (24 September 2026): hold pending evidence; do not pay a booking advance yet.** The RERA identity and brochure match, the five-tower plan is approved, and the typical-floor saleable total reconciles. The uploaded 2022 title opinion covers only 2.875 acres while the approval covers 6.136 net acres; a later press allegation questions survey boundaries, without a primary finding located. Independent whole-site title/boundary work and unit-specific mortgage releases are the decision gates. This is research triage, not a legal or engineering clearance. [Detailed findings](rera-findings.md) · [Source log](sources.md).

## What would change the decision

1. **Before money:** independent full-parcel title/boundary and land-classification opinion addressing Survey 121 versus 117/6/122; current litigation/FIR status and official HMDA/revenue confirmation. The uploaded title report is insufficient for this purpose. [Title report](sources/tgrera-title-search-report.pdf); [2026 allegation](https://global360tv.in/the-namishree-aria-land-scam/).
2. **For the chosen unit:** current EC, Bajaj lender NOC, HMDA mortgage release where relevant, owner/developer area-share allocation and exactly who signs the deed; written current RERA collection account. [Permit](sources/tgrera-building-permit.pdf), [finance disclosure](sources/tgrera-encumbrance-2.pdf).
3. **For value and elder suitability:** certified carpet area and all-in quote; promised possession date; actual passenger/service lift count and backup load; water supply and charges; approved parking bay; ambulance and daily-errand field checks. [Brochure](sources/namishree-aria-brochure-2026.pdf), [official filing](sources/tgrera-filing-retrieved-2026-09-24.html).

If the first two sets are independently resolved and unit economics are competitive on **carpet area**, it becomes a reasonable **site visit** candidate. Until then, the title/charge uncertainty outweighs the amenity pitch.
