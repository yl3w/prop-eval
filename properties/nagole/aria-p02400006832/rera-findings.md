# ARIA — official record findings

As of 24 September 2026. This is a read of a historical public RERA print (search result last modified **26 August 2023**), its uploaded HMDA permission and title documents, and the [certificate](sources/tgrera-certificate-2023-09-01.pdf). Current status needs a fresh signed progress report and direct authority confirmation.

## Identity and scope

- **Verified:** P02400006832 is **ARIA**, not a different Nagole project or phase. Registered promoter: **NAMISHREE PROJECTS**, a **partnership**. Site: Survey 121/P, Thatti Annaram village, Abdullapurmet mandal, Ranga Reddy district, PIN 500068. [Official certificate](sources/tgrera-certificate-2023-09-01.pdf), [detailed print](sources/tgrera-filing-retrieved-2026-09-24.html). The builder's [brochure](sources/namishree-aria-brochure-2026.pdf) names ARIA at Nagole and prints the same RERA ID.
- **Entity to reconcile:** The brochure calls it Namishree, while [Housing](https://housing.com/in/buy/projects/page/306247-namishree-aria-by-namishree-infrastructure-and-project-pvt-ltd-in-nagole) attributes it to “Namishree Infrastructure And Project Pvt Ltd.” This is not the registered partnership. Obtain legal names and signing authority on the unit agreement, sale deed, invoices and collection account.
- **Approval:** HMDA permission no. **001610/BP/HMDA/0341/GHT/2023**, dated 10 July 2023, for five residential blocks and a ground-plus-five amenities block. Block 1: two cellars + ground + 25 upper floors; Blocks 2–5: ground + 25 upper floors. This explains 25 residential unit floors versus 26 storeys including ground; listing claims of “28 floors” should be checked against the approved drawings. [HMDA permission p1](sources/tgrera-building-permit.pdf), [approved plans](sources/tgrera-sanctioned-plan-1.pdf).
- **Dates conflict:** Certificate validity ends **8 July 2028**; detailed filing says proposed completion **8 November 2028**. Broker sites often print July 2028. Obtain written RERA-approved completion/possession date, current extension status and agreement date. The 2023 permission says plans valid for five years from permission issue, also making any post-July 2028 work/renewal status material. [Certificate](sources/tgrera-certificate-2023-09-01.pdf), [filing](sources/tgrera-filing-retrieved-2026-09-24.html), [permission p3](sources/tgrera-building-permit.pdf).

## Land, title and funding: priority checks

- **Registered land:** gross **24,907.34 m²**, deduction **80.24 m²**, net **24,827.10 m² = 6.136 net acres**. The HMDA permission says the 80.24 m² was gifted for a Master Plan road affected area / peripheral road / open, utility and internal road areas. It is **0.32%** of gross. The general RERA field combines road widening, FTL and nala; the permission's gift-deed description is more specific but does not establish a no-flood-risk finding. [Filing](sources/tgrera-filing-retrieved-2026-09-24.html), [permission p3](sources/tgrera-building-permit.pdf).
- **Material title-scope mismatch:** The uploaded advocate's 21 May 2022 opinion opens and concludes on only **2 acres 35 guntas = 2.875 acres** in Survey 121, about **47%** of the 6.136-acre approved net site. Other deeds/development agreements are uploaded, but this particular report does **not** give a whole-site title opinion. Its final page says it examined photocopies, did not certify their authenticity, did not check physical boundaries, recommends a topographic survey, and did not empirically search for litigation or acquisition. Its listed observations also call for original-document verification and some missing EC/land-use records. [Title report pp1,14–15](sources/tgrera-title-search-report.pdf); [permission p1](sources/tgrera-building-permit.pdf). Obtain an independent 2026 advocate report for **all 24,827.10 m²**, survey subdivision map/FMB, government demarcation, land classification and current court/revenue searches.
- **2026 allegation, unverified:** [Bharatha Sakthi](https://epaper.bharathasakthi.com/media-webp/2026-03/bs-final-14.pdf) and a [republication](https://global360tv.in/the-namishree-aria-land-scam/) allege part of construction crosses into surveys 117/6 and 122 and mention an FIR. I found no primary FIR, final boundary order or court determination; the republication even misstates the RERA ID. This is **not a finding of wrongdoing**. The gap between the uploaded title opinion and approved parcel is enough to require independent boundary/title work before payment. Ask for certified FIR/status if one exists, land survey, and written HMDA/revenue response.
- **Mortgage / lender:** The HMDA permission p2 lists three 2023 registered mortgages: 5% built-up area for impact fee installment (6,317.75 m²), 10% regular mortgage (12,635.45 m²), and 5% for NALA conversion (6,317.75 m², including amenities area). These are distinct obligations and may overlap in physical unit allocation; do **not** sum to a blanket 20% of units. Its plan labels some lower-floor units as mortgaged. Separately, an uploaded [promoter disclosure](sources/tgrera-encumbrance-2.pdf) states **₹100 crore construction finance from Bajaj Housing Finance** and an IndusInd “ARIA collection account.” Obtain a current 30-year EC, complete mortgage-unit schedule, lender NOC/release for the chosen flat, and approved payment instructions. [HMDA permission p2](sources/tgrera-building-permit.pdf), [plan](sources/tgrera-sanctioned-plan-1.pdf).
- **RERA accounts:** The 2023 filing shows a 100% Kotak account but blank 70% separate and 30% transaction account fields. The later uploaded Bajaj/IndusInd collection disclosure is a different account setup, with no date visible on the scan. The current designated RERA account and collection waterfall need written reconciliation; a blank historical field is not proof of current noncompliance. [Filing](sources/tgrera-filing-retrieved-2026-09-24.html), [promoter disclosure](sources/tgrera-encumbrance-2.pdf).
- **Landowner interest:** The filing's landowner/investor promoter field says “No”; the uploaded 2022 development agreement names landowner **C. Amarender Reddy** and the promoter partnership as developer. A landowner need not be separately registered as promoter for every structure; what matters here is the executed area-share/deed chain, chosen unit's allocation and who signs the deed. [Filing](sources/tgrera-filing-retrieved-2026-09-24.html), [development agreement upload](sources/tgrera-title-347720.pdf).

## Units, areas, progress and uploads

- **Scale:** five blocks × 25 residential floors × five flats = **625 flats**, 125 per block. Density **625 / 6.136 = 101.9 flats per net acre**. This is project density on the approved parcel; verify no other phase uses it. [Filing](sources/tgrera-filing-retrieved-2026-09-24.html), [brochure pp22–24](sources/namishree-aria-brochure-2026.pdf).
- **Area reconciliation:** Each RERA floor row shows **five apartments and 933.66 m² saleable area**. Converting gives **10,050.0 sq ft for the row**, exactly the brochure's A/B/C typical-floor mix of **four 2,100 sq ft + one 1,650 sq ft**. Thus it is a **floor-group total**, not a 10,050-sq-ft individual flat. The RERA public print does not show a reliable **unit-level carpet area**, nor does the 2026 brochure publish the carpet-to-built-up-to-saleable chain. Get the chosen flat's certified RERA carpet area and floor plan. [Filing](sources/tgrera-filing-retrieved-2026-09-24.html), [brochure p23](sources/namishree-aria-brochure-2026.pdf).
- **Price:** Listing asks span roughly ₹99 lakh–₹1.36 crore for 1,650/2,100 sq ft. These are marketing asks, not an all-in written unit quote or registered transactions. Accordingly **all-in price / RERA carpet area**, efficiency **carpet / marketed saleable**, and transaction-vs-ask discount are **unavailable**. Obtain flat-specific price sheet including floor/view premiums, GST, parking, club/corpus, maintenance advance, statutory charges, and deed area basis. [Listing links](sources.md).
- **Parking:** Builder promises one bay for 1,650 sq ft and two for 2,100 sq ft. If the illustrated four-large/one-small floor mix is repeated across all 125 residential floors, demand would be **1,125 bays**; this is an illustrative calculation, **not** a verified unit mix or bay count. The filing field “Covered Parking (In Numbers)” is **37,237**, impossible as a physical bay count for 625 homes and likely an area entered in a count field; Block A also lists 37,327 m² total parking area. Do not infer parking surplus. Get the sanctioned parking schedule, actual bay numbering, visitor/EV allocation and apartment-specific allotment. [Filing](sources/tgrera-filing-retrieved-2026-09-24.html), [builder page](https://namishree.com/projects/3-bhk-flats-for-sale-in-nagole-hyderabad-aria/).
- **Bookings and progress:** The 2023 row-level booked flags and work-task percentages read zero; the portal search result has no newer last-modified date. They are **historical**, cannot establish 2026 unsold inventory or construction status, and should not be described as current zero progress. Ask for latest quarterly RERA update, independent architect/engineer certificate, lender disbursement progress and dated site photos. The builder has a [July 2026 update video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O82eN3ZC1-A), which is marketing, not an engineer certificate.
- **Experience:** The filing says past experience “Yes” and lists **Saffron Elite**, Nallagandla, 87 flats, proposed 19 June 2019 and actual 17 August 2022. This is a prior-project schedule gap in the 2023 disclosure, not proof of a delay at ARIA. Group-level counts on listing sites are not the same legal promoter's declared record. [Filing](sources/tgrera-filing-retrieved-2026-09-24.html).
- **Uploads:** Sanctioned plans, building permission and title report are uploaded. The 2023 print marks society-formation status, conveyance, overall architect-completion and occupancy certificate as not uploaded. With completion proposed in 2028, absence of an OC is expected at this stage; it also means **no occupancy assumption**. [Filing](sources/tgrera-filing-retrieved-2026-09-24.html).

2. Certified whole-site cadastral/FMB/topographic overlay for 24,827.10 m², all relevant survey subdivisions, forest/revenue classification, and independent current title/litigation opinion with the newspaper allegation specifically addressed.
