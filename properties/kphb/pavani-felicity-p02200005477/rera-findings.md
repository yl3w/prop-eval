## Units, areas, bookings and parking

- The apartment table in print pp. 6–14 contains **273 BHK rows** by direct row count. One row (`1212`) says 2BHK despite otherwise 3BHK marketing, and row `1704` is printed with floor ID 15. Ask the developer to reconcile these apparent data-entry errors and any amenity-area rows before relying on an inventory extract.
- **91/273 BHK rows (33.3%)** have booked flag 1 in this print. This is a portal-field count, **not verified customer absorption**: 54 rows show `Mortgage=True`, 13 of those are also marked booked; three area-share landowner-promoters may hold allocations. The separate parking field says 88 booked of 485 proposed bays. Request a current, signed flat-by-flat sold / landowner / mortgage / unsold schedule.
- 273 homes / 2.194 **net** acres = **124.4 homes per net acre** (gross-land figure 114.3 per acre). This assumes the net RERA parcel covers all 273 homes and no outside blocks use its services. [2023 brochure](sources/pavani-felicity-brochure-mirror-2023-10-05.pdf), pp. 3, 23–26, depicts one connected building and 273 homes.
- 485 proposed covered parking bays / 273 homes = **1.78 bays per home in aggregate**; this does not establish any flat's entitlement, bay geometry, visitor allocation, or whether amenities/commercial uses share parking. Print p. 4 says open parking 0. Require sanctioned cellar plans and a written allocation for the chosen unit.
- The filing labels unit figures **“Saleable Area (in sqmts)”**, not carpet. The [2023 brochure](sources/pavani-felicity-brochure-mirror-2023-10-05.pdf), p. 26, labels the corresponding numbers in sq ft; the [developer project page](https://pavaniinfra.com/project/pavani-felicity) calls the range super built-up. The developer landing page instead labels the *same* range “Carpet Area” in its configuration table. A unit-level RERA carpet schedule is still required.
