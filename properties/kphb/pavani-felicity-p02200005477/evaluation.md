## What would change the decision

1. **Before any advance:** official certificate and latest dated RERA filing/QPR; authenticated sanctioned building and cellar plans; survey with the 785.50 sq m road/FTL/nala split; title/encumbrance and registered area-share allocation for the chosen flat; exact separate RERA bank accounts.
2. **For the exact offered flat:** signed area schedule with RERA carpet, marketed/saleable area and deed basis; all-in dated cost sheet (base, floor rise, parking, GST/stamp/registration, corpus and other charges). Then compute carpet efficiency and all-in carpet rate.
3. **For a parent's home:** physically verify lift count and 8:30am worst wait, generator-supported lift/lighting and flat circuits, municipal versus bore/tanker supply and charges, bathroom/stair access, 6pm ambulance path, monsoon cellar/access condition, nearby errands and 2am cardiac route.

**Why hold:** The project is substantially built structurally but the supplied progress table still shows key operating/OC work at 30–50% on an undated snapshot. RERA completion dates and developer possession copy disagree; carpet/price, bank separation, water source, title allocations and senior-critical building operations remain unproven. A site visit becomes worthwhile once the developer supplies the first two document sets above; until then the brochure alone cannot support a purchase decision.
