## Legal and title issue that spans all phases

In [CC/66/2024, 12 March 2025](sources/tgrera-amenities-order-2025-03-12.pdf), Telangana RERA held that the entire amenities block forms common area to be conveyed to the flat owners' association, found a Section 17 violation, and imposed a ₹10,60,318 penalty. The order also directed adherence to the sanctioned plan and solar lighting/water heating. The promoter argued that parts of the ground, first and second floors were its exclusive commercial property; the order rejected that treatment. It described claimed third-party interests in upper floors too.

The [25 June 2025 order](sources/tgrera-phase-iii-rejection-2025-06-25.pdf) rejected **a prior G/H registration application**, `REA02200085222`, because the draft sale agreement again asserted exclusive promoter rights over significant amenities floors, public access and parking, and made transfer subject to court proceedings. It records the promoter's High Court challenge, **WP 13875/2025**. The later Phase III filing/ID indicates that a subsequent registration exists; the 2025 rejection does **not** prove the present registration was rejected or cancelled. The supplied print says “Litigations related to the project? No” in each phase, which conflicts with the official orders' account of an amenities dispute and writ. This may be a stale or narrowly completed field, but must be reconciled in writing.

**Before any advance:** obtain the current Phase III certificate, current filed agreement for sale including clubhouse clause 34 or its successor, sanctioned/revised master plan and amenities schedule, current status/orders in WP 13875/2025, proof of compliance or stay of the March RERA order, all OC/part-OC copies with covered blocks, registered conveyance to the association (or a documented reason not yet complete), and the commercial/public entrance and parking rights. Have an independent Hyderabad property lawyer compare these with the proposed sale deed. The project has multiple named area-share landowner-promoters in each phase; get the specific seller's allocation, JDA/supplementary share agreements, chain of title, latest EC and mortgage release for the exact flat and UDS.

## Review evidence, with its limits

The [Google Maps place](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Vazhraa+Prathik/@17.5152889,78.3862091,17z/data=!4m8!3m7!1s0x3bcb91ed6897af17:0x438d7d029413ee11!8m2!3d17.5152889!4d78.3862091!9m1!1b1!16s%2Fg%2F11gmjmwl4b) at the registered Nizampet site showed **4.3/5 from 615 reviews** on 24 September 2026 (345 five-star, 177 four-star, 44 three-star, 9 two-star, 40 one-star). Its category is apartment complex, but it also displays sales-office hours, developer URL and builder phone: the review population may mix residents, visitors and buyers. **It cannot be scored as 615 verified resident accounts.** The signed-out view explicitly said it was limited and blocked full sort/search via sign-in prompt. Text fraction, monthly bursts, yearly trend, recent lowest reviews, keyword result counts and median reviewer history were therefore **not established**. No separate verified builder listing was audited.

Visible sample only: a reviewer claiming over a year of residence (three months before this review) praised essential amenities and Vijetha; a 4-star Local Guide noted walkways and nearby errands; a 1-star review edited about a year ago alleged cracks, leaks, poor maintenance response and water connection problems for a newer block. These are unverified personal accounts; the low-rated maintenance/water allegation deserves direct resident and logbook follow-up, especially for elderly occupancy. [MagicBricks' 4.8/5 from four project ratings](https://www.magicbricks.com/vazhraa-prathik-nizampet-hyderabad-pdpid-4d4235313136373631) includes agent comments and is a separate, much smaller feed. Do not merge it with Google or use locality ratings as project ratings. For a fuller audit, use a signed-in review export or manual newest/lowest text sample, identify actual residents, and search water/lift/maintenance in **that** feed; a zero-result search would mean only “not found in the accessed feed.”

## Short list of evidence that could change the decision

1. **Amenity rights and litigation:** current High Court/RERA status, revised filed sale agreement, association conveyance and commercial/parking separation plan.
2. **Tower-specific legal delivery:** current registration certificates and actual OCs for A–F; Phase III certificate and current engineer progress against **18 April 2030**; title/EC/landowner allocation/mortgage release for the offered unit.
3. **Price and usable area:** unit's registered carpet, builder brochure/plan mapping, itemized all-in quote, proposed deed area and matched government deeds. Without these, no carpet-rate comparison is valid.
4. **Senior household operations:** actual working lifts and backup load test, municipal/bore/tanker charges, bathroom dimensions, ambulance route, cellar flood history, and tower-wise occupancy/maintenance accounts.

**Phase-specific call:** A–C: **hold pending OC and amenity/title resolution** despite apparent occupancy. D–F: **conditional visit**, because the print shows 100% tasks and OC links; still obtain and read the underlying OCs, amenity terms and actual operating records before commitment. G–H: **hold pending evidence**, with substantial filed unfinished work, no OC upload, a 2030 registered date and a direct conflict with developer 2026 possession marketing. The shared amenity dispute affects all three.
